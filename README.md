# Group Item Tracker
### A RuneLite plugin to help Group Ironmen keep track of shared items in their possession.<br>
<img width="1110" height="385" alt="Example gif" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7a8bc664-62e4-4aa9-add4-f2de46ad9ec3" />


## Tracking Items
Items can be tracked from the bank or group storage interface while in <b>edit-mode</b>.<br>
<b>Edit-mode</b> can be toggled by right-clicking the <b>Group Storage</b> button in the bank, or the <b>Back to bank</b> button in group storage.<br>
<img width="415" height="92" alt="Edit-mode menu example" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ae5f9db6-de99-406c-8dee-d32408fdb5c5" />

 <b>Edit-mode</b> adds a <b>Start-tracking/Stop-tracking</b> menu option to items in the bank and group storage.<br>
<img width="673" height="83" alt="Start-tracking example" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/732f169a-ef48-4dc2-8fbf-84c7519b6309" />

Alternatively, you can stop tracking an item at any time by clicking the corresponding ✕ button in the sidebar.

## Features
- Remembers the location of tracked items across the bank, equipment, and inventory containers.
- Variant aware - a charged and uncharged blowpipe are treated as the same item.
- Filter tracked items by searching '/g' in the bank and group storage.
- Copy tracked items to your clipboard to quickly share with group members.
- Draw outlines around tracked items in the bank and group storage.
- Reorder the sidebar panel, or turn it off entirely.

## Notes
- 100% human generated code.
- No network calls, limited to items in your personal possession.
- Feature complete for my own use cases, though I'm open to suggestions.
- I've tried to keep things lightweight but there's always room for improvement, particularly with Swing.
